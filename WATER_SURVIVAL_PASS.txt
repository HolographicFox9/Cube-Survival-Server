HOSTL Game 648 / Server 576 / Rules 602

WATER + HYDRATION PASS

- Added 26 seeded ponds to every HOSTL world. Pond placement is shared between offline and multiplayer generation.
- Ponds use the thick-outline, flat-color art style and reserve their footprint so normal scenery is not intentionally placed inside them.
- Every run now starts with Fist + Bucket in the first two hotbar slots.
- Bucket begins empty. Equip it and use it near a pond to fill it. Use a full bucket to drink.
- A full bucket restores 70 hydration.
- Added a permanent Hydration meter above the hotbar/Skill bar.
- Hydration starts at 100 and drains at 0.22 per second while alive.
- At 35 hydration and below, on-foot speed progressively falls; at 0 hydration the player moves at 60% normal on-foot speed.
- Pet-mounted speed is not reduced by the rider's hydration.
- Multiplayer hydration, bucket state, filling distance, drinking, and dehydration movement limits are authoritative on the server.
- Ponds cannot be harvested/destroyed as normal resources.
- Hydration and the empty bucket reset on a new run/respawn.
